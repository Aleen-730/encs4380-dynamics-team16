Notes – how the values are calculated

  • y(τ), y(3τ): read from the step curve at t = τ and t = 3τ.
  • t63, t95: first time the step curve reaches 63.2% and 95% of AK.
  • Steady-state error: x − y/K at the end of the ramp (t = 25 s).
  • Ramp lag: steady-state error divided by the ramp slope A.
  • Log slope: [ln e(3τ) − ln e(τ)] / (2τ), where e = (AK − y)/AK.
