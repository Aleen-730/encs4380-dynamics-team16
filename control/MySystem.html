2. The Spec and Why It Matters

Requirement Limit Why it matters for the micromouse
Overshoot ≤ % The wheel must not speed past the target, or the mouse hits the wall in a short maze cell.
Settling time (2 %) ≤ s A fast speed loop lets the mouse accelerate and turn in a short distance.
Steady-state error ≤ % Two wheels at the same speed mean the mouse drives straight, and distance by encoder stays accurate.
Damping ratio ζ 0.6 to 0.8 Practical range from the lecture: fast, with small overshoot.
PWM command 0 to 100 % The driver cannot give more than 100 %. If u hits the limit, the controller is not working as designed.

3. Controller Design (PI, digital)

For the plant K/(τs + 1) with a PI controller the closed loop is second order (same formulas as validation check 2 of Homework 2):

ωn = √(K·Ki / τ)    ζ = (1 + K·Kp) / (2√(τ·K·Ki))

We choose ζ and ωn, then solve for the gains:

Ki = ωn² · τ / K    Kp = (2ζωnτ − 1) / K
